Five Claude Code hooks on a real repo, and the two that never fired until a one-line matcher fix.
Demo repo (agentshipped/notes-api): https://github.com/agentshipped/notes-api

I wired a guard, a syntax check, a stop marker, an audit log and a change log, then counted what each one did. Three of three reads of .env were blocked; two hooks sat at zero because of their matchers.
You can copy the stdin JSON contract (exit code 2 plus a sentence on stderr), a per-hook count log, and the matcher fix: Bash, not bash, and Edit|Write, not Write.
Not just JavaScript: the same hook contract works in any stack, e.g. py_compile instead of node --check, and ruff or mypy instead of a JS linter.
Tools: Claude Code, hooks (PreToolUse, PostToolUse, Stop), jq, node --check, sed
